OUR PROCESS

How a build moves from meeting to move-in

Five stages, start to finish. Scroll through, or just wait a moment — it moves on its own.

01 / MEET

Meet

A site visit and requirement discussion — plot size, budget, layout preferences and the package tier that fits.

02 / DESIGN

Design

Architectural, structural and electrical drawings plus a 3D elevation, finalized and signed off with you.

03 / SANCTION

Submit for sanction

Drawings submitted to the local planning authority for plan sanction and approvals before any excavation begins.

04 / MOBILIZE

Mobilization

Site engineer deployed, materials procured, and temporary power/water arranged ahead of ground-breaking.

05 / EXECUTE

Execution

Milestone-based construction from foundation to handover, with weekly architect visits and sign-offs at every slab.

SCOPE OF WORK

What's built in, and what you'll need to arrange

Transparency on scope avoids disputes later — here's the exact split from our standard agreement.

Included in every package

  • Site engineer/supervisor present at all times
  • Weekly architect site visits
  • Structural engineer visit before roof slab casting
  • Architectural, structural and electrical drawings + 3D elevation
  • 5,000L underground sump and 1,000L overhead tank
  • Safety and Security Grills for all windows

Arranged separately by client

  • Temporary power connection for the construction period
  • Water and power supply during construction (at no cost to us)
  • BESCOM pole connection, KEB meter & liaison charges
  • Civic authority permissions and approval charges
  • Borewell, motor pumps and water-level controller
  • Sanitary line connection to BWSSB main chamber, shed cost
