SCOPE OF WORK

What's built in, and what you'll need to arrange

Transparency on scope avoids disputes later — here's the exact split from our standard agreement.

Included in every package

  • Site engineer/supervisor present at all times
  • Weekly architect site visits
  • Structural engineer visit before roof slab casting
  • Architectural, structural and electrical drawings + 3D elevation
  • 5,000L underground sump and 1,000L overhead tank
  • MS staircase railing and bathroom/bedroom grouting

Arranged separately by client

  • Temporary power connection for the construction period
  • Water and power supply during construction (at no cost to us)
  • BESCOM pole connection, KEB meter & liaison charges
  • Civic authority permissions and approval charges
  • Borewell, motor pumps and water-level controller
  • Sanitary line connection to BWSSB main chamber, shed cost
